
# 📊 Business Insights 360 - Comprehensive Power BI Dashboard

An enterprise-grade, multi-functional business intelligence analytics tool developed as part of the Codebasics Power BI Project. This interactive dashboard centralizes core metrics across Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive views to drive data-backed decision-making.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

**Business Insights 360** is designed to solve critical business visibility challenges for a fictional hardware manufacturing company (AtliQ Hardware). It provides comprehensive tracking of profitability, regional performance, customer behavior, and supply chain accuracy.

## 📑 Dashboard Architecture & Views

The application features a centralized home navigation portal linking to five core business modules:

1. **Finance View:** Profit & Loss statement tracking across customers, products, and countries over any custom time period[cite: 1].
2. **Sales View:** Customer-level performance evaluated through Net Sales, Gross Margin, and profitability/growth matrices[cite: 1].
3. **Marketing View:** Product-level performance analysis mapped against key commercial metrics and growth quadrants[cite: 1].
4. **Supply Chain View:** Forecast accuracy, net error metrics, and risk profiles (Out-of-Stock / Excess Inventory tracking) categorized by product and customer segments[cite: 1].
5. **Executive View:** High-level executive summary consolidating vital top-line metrics and cross-dimensional insights[cite: 1].

## 💡 Key Insights & Recommendations

* **High Regional Dominance:** APAC and North America drive the majority of top-line revenue, while European markets experience tighter margin constraints.
* **Operational Expense Pressures:** Despite healthy gross margins (~38.08%), heavy operational expenses pull net profitability into negative territory, highlighting the need for cost optimization[cite: 1].
* **Inventory Balancing:** Supply chain metrics reveal specific risks regarding excess inventory (EI) in storage and peripheral segments, requiring tighter alignment with demand forecasting.
